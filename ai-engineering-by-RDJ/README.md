## 📌 About

This is the AI engineering track of [`code-with-RDJ`](../README.md): the move from "can write Python" to "can build and evaluate AI systems". It is the primary career target of the whole repository.

- **Foundation:** the [`python-by-RDJ`](../python-by-RDJ/README.md) lab (5 modules and 3 projects, all complete and tested) is the prerequisite. FastAPI is the next step there, and this lab starts after it.
- **What belongs here:** services, retrieval systems, agents, and — above all — evaluations. A demo is not evidence; a measured result is.
- **What does not belong here:** anything that claims a skill level or a ranking. Targets turn green only with proof.
